**MHT-CET College Finder & Cutoff Preference System**

**Problem Statement** **:** 

Students aiming for MHT-CET, which is the common entrance examination for engineering colleges in Maharashtra, should choose the colleges and streams of engineering they could consider based on their percentiles and admission categories. The process of comparing previous cut-off percentages for admissions in various colleges and streams can be tedious.

The MHT CET College Finder and Cutoff Preferences System is a Java-based software meant to facilitate search for engineering colleges/branches by students based on the historical cutoffs. The system categorizes the options into Safe, Target, or Dream based on admission chances as per the predetermined criteria.

The users can filter the search results, access the relevant cutoff details, set up a preference list, and export their preferences in a text file. The administrators can maintain the details about the college, branch, and cutoff and import the cutoff details in CSV files.

Java Swing is used for designing the GUI, JDBC for connecting to the database, and MySQL for storing and processing the information.

The tool offers predictions based on past data. It does not assure admission nor does it replace the MHT-CET CAP process.

**Objectives** **:** 

Provide a central mechanism to search for engineering colleges and branches.
Compare student percentiles with historical cutoffs where applicable.
Classify college-branch choices into Safe, Target or Dream based on pre-agreed criteria.
Let students have the ability to save, sort and export their college choices.
Let the administrator maintain college and cutoff information.
Provide an ability for importing and validating cutoff information in CSV format.
Implement database normalization, SQL queries, stored procedures, functions, triggers, views and transaction management.
Use object-oriented concepts from Java like inheritance, interface, collection, exception handling, JDBC and multithreading.

**Scope** **:** 

This system utilizes a representative sample of historical MHT-CET CAP-round cutoff scores. This helps students to authenticate themselves, search for colleges, manage their preferences and maintain administrative data.

The reliability of the information generated will depend on the accuracy of the historical data available.
